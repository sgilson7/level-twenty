Every picture in `web/art/` is drawn by `tools/art.py` from TikZ written in this repository; the look follows `Sam_Comm_final.png` in Sam Gilson's vagrancy repository, and no pixel of it is copied. Every sound is synthesized by the page from `data/sounds.json`, and the 3D figures and scenery are built by the core from boxes, spheres, cones and cylinders.
